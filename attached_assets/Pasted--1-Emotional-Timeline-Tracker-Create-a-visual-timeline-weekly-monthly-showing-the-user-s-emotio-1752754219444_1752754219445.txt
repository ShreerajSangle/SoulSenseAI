🌱 1. Emotional Timeline Tracker
Create a visual timeline (weekly/monthly) showing the user’s emotional journey across sessions.

Use mood logs, journal sentiments, and AI sentiment detection to color-code the graph.

Users can click on points to revisit chat excerpts, goals, or reflections from that day.

🧭 2. Persona-Guided Pathways
Let users choose from guided tracks (e.g., Healing from Burnout, Finding Purpose, Building Confidence).

Each persona offers a themed 7-day or 21-day journey with tailored prompts, meditations, and reflections.

Progress is shown as a calming path, flower bloom, or chakra map.

🔁 3. Daily SoulSense Loop
A gentle daily ritual powered by the AI:

Mood check-in (emoji or short phrase)

One reflection question

Suggested activity (breathing, journaling, etc.)

Affirmation of the day

Can be customized by persona tone (Maya's loop is soft and meditative, Marcus’s is structured and uplifting).

🧠 4. Conversational Memory Recall
Allow personas to remember key user moments, like “You told me last week you were nervous about the presentation. How did it go?”

Stored lightly in memory and brought back in a natural way after 2–3 days.

💌 5. Emotion-Aware Responses
When a user expresses sadness, stress, or joy, adjust the persona’s tone and reply length dynamically.

Example: If a user types “I feel lost,” Dr. Sarah could reply with a slower, empathetic 3-line message and offer to journal or breathe.

🎭 6. Interactive Emotion Cards
After a big emotional message, show optional cards:

“Name this feeling” 🧠

“Want to explore this deeper?” 🌀

“Add this to your journal?” 📓

🪄 7. Magic Phrase Recognition
If a user types something like “I need help” or “Can you motivate me?” — trigger a special supportive flow from Marcus, Alex, or Maya instantly.

This makes it feel like the AI gets them in real time.

📦 8. Soft Archive & Memory Lane
Let users revisit a calming archive: “Moments you overcame,” “Thoughts that healed you,” “Affirmations that worked.”

Filter by persona, emotion, or goal type.

🎨 9. User-Editable Persona Layers
Allow users to:

Rename the personas (“Coach M” instead of Marcus)

Choose their preferred tone: Cheerful, Soothing, Wise, etc.

Select themes, fonts, or music styles for background ambiance.

🔐 10. Personal Safe Space Mode
A toggle that slows down all responses, dims the interface, and activates Maya’s most grounding flow.

Includes music, meditation, and a “do not disturb” bubble.

Ideal for anxious moments.
