# SoulSenseAI
SoulSense AI is a sophisticated mental health and emotional wellness
  application designed to provide a supportive and therapeutic
  experience through AI-powered personas. It offers a safe space for
  users to engage in meaningful conversations, track their emotional
  state, set personal goals, and reflect on their journey.

  Key Features:

   * AI-Powered Chat: Engage in conversations with different AI personas,
     each with a unique personality and therapeutic approach.
   * Emotional Analytics: Visualize your emotional trends and gain
     insights into your mental well-being through an analytics dashboard.
   * Goal Setting: Create and manage personal goals to foster growth and
     track your progress.
   * Digital Diary: A private and secure diary to record your thoughts,
     feelings, and experiences.
   * Mood Tracking: Log your mood throughout the day to build a
     comprehensive emotional timeline.
   * Breathing Exercises: Guided breathing exercises to help you relax and
      manage stress.
   * User Profile: A personal space to manage your information and
     preferences.

  Tech Stack:

  This project is a full-stack application built with a modern and
  scalable architecture.

   * Frontend: React, Radix UI & shadcn/ui, Tailwind CSS, Wouter, React
     Query, Vite
   * Backend: FastAPI (Python) & Node.js with Express, Anthropic Claude &
     OpenAI
   * Database: PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM & SQLAlchemy
